## Project Overview

This project presents a comprehensive analysis of an e-commerce business performance dataset to identify the key factors affecting revenue growth, marketing efficiency, customer acquisition, retention, and product category performance.

The objective of this project is to transform raw business data into actionable insights that support strategic decision-making.

The analysis focuses on:

- Revenue growth trends
- Marketing channel performance
- Customer acquisition efficiency
- Conversion rate optimization
- Customer retention behavior
- Product category contribution
- Business growth recommendations

---

# Business Problem

The company is experiencing increasing website traffic and marketing investment; however, revenue growth is slowing down.

The key business questions explored in this project are:

- Is increasing marketing spend generating proportional revenue growth?
- Are we attracting high-quality customers or only increasing traffic volume?
- Which marketing channels provide the best return?
- Why is customer retention declining?
- Which product categories drive revenue and customer volume?
- How can the business achieve sustainable growth?

---

# Project Objectives

The main objectives of this analysis are:

- Diagnose reasons behind slowing revenue growth
- Evaluate marketing channel efficiency using key performance indicators
- Analyze customer acquisition and retention trends
- Identify high-performing product categories
- Recommend strategies for improving profitability and customer lifetime value

---

# Dataset Analysis Framework

The project evaluates business performance using the following KPIs:

| KPI | Description |
|---|---|
| Revenue | Total business income generated |
| Ad Spend | Marketing investment across channels |
| Sessions | Website traffic volume |
| Orders | Completed customer purchases |
| Conversion Rate | Percentage of visitors converting into customers |
| Average Order Value (AOV) | Average revenue per order |
| ROAS | Return generated from advertising spend |
| CAC | Cost required to acquire a customer |
| Retention Rate | Percentage of customers making repeat purchases |

# Key Findings

## 1. Revenue Growth vs Marketing Investment

The analysis identified a gap between advertising investment and revenue growth.

![](https://github.com/maidulhassan-collab/ecommerce-marketing-performance-analytics/blob/main/Media%20files/Increasing%20Spend%20vs%20Stagment%20Revenue.png)

### Findings:

- Revenue increased approximately *9.2%*
- Advertising spend increased approximately *131.2%*

### Insight:

The business is entering a diminishing return phase where additional marketing spending is generating lower incremental revenue.

---

# 2. Traffic Growth but Conversion Decline

Website traffic increased significantly, but customer conversion efficiency declined.

![](https://github.com/maidulhassan-collab/ecommerce-marketing-performance-analytics/blob/main/Media%20files/Monthly%20Conversion%20Rate%20Decline.png)

### Findings:

- Sessions increased from *420,000 to 680,000 (+62%)*
- Conversion rate decreased from *2.19% to 1.54%*

### Insight:

The business is attracting more visitors, but traffic quality and purchase intent are declining.

---

# 3. Marketing Channel Performance

## Best Performing Channels

![](https://github.com/maidulhassan-collab/ecommerce-marketing-performance-analytics/blob/main/Media%20files/ROAS%20by%20Channel.png)

### Email Marketing

- ROAS: *9.33*
- Strongest efficiency channel
- Opportunity for CRM and loyalty campaigns


### Google Ads

- Strong paid acquisition performance
- Better customer acquisition efficiency


## Weak Performing Channel

### Affiliate Marketing

- Higher customer acquisition cost
- Low return compared with investment

### Recommendation:

Reevaluate affiliate spending and focus budget on high-performing channels.

---

# 4. Customer Retention Challenge

The analysis identifies a retention problem.

![](https://github.com/maidulhassan-collab/ecommerce-marketing-performance-analytics/blob/main/Media%20files/customer%20retention%20trend.png)

### Findings:

- New customer acquisition increased
- 60-day repeat purchase rate declined:

*15.9% → 10.8%*

### Insight:

The business is acquiring customers but failing to maximize customer lifetime value.

---

# 5. Product Category Performance

![](https://github.com/maidulhassan-collab/ecommerce-marketing-performance-analytics/blob/main/Media%20files/category%20Mix.png)

## Fashion Category

Strength:

- Highest order volume
- Strong customer acquisition potential


## Electronics Category

Strength:

- Highest revenue contribution
- Highest Average Order Value

Performance:

- Revenue contribution: *9.8M BDT*
- Average Order Value: *3,161 BDT*

### Strategic Insight:

Use Fashion for customer acquisition and Electronics for revenue growth.

# Strategic Recommendations

## 1. Optimize Marketing Investment

Shift budget toward channels with:

- Higher ROAS
- Lower CAC
- Better conversion performance

---

## 2. Focus on Customer Retention

Recommended actions:

- CRM campaigns
- Email loyalty programs
- Personalized recommendations
- Repeat purchase incentives

---

## 3. Improve Traffic Quality

Instead of focusing only on increasing visitors:

- Target high-intent customers
- Improve landing page experience
- Optimize conversion funnels

---

## 4. Strengthen High-Value Categories

Strategy:

- Promote Electronics for revenue growth
- Use Fashion campaigns for customer acquisition
- Create product bundles to increase AOV
